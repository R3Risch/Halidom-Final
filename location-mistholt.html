Mistholt — The Veiled City

Perched along the rugged northern coastline of Valkhiem, Mistholt is a sprawling port city perpetually shrouded in thick, rolling fog. Known throughout the region as the Veiled City, its towering stone architecture, narrow cobbled streets, and bustling harbors are often obscured beneath a blanket of mist. Despite its gloomy appearance, Mistholt serves as the political and economic heart of Valkhiem, welcoming merchants, sailors, and travelers from across the seas. Ancient traditions and modern ambitions coexist within its walls, while rumors of forgotten magic and hidden dealings linger in the fog. To outsiders, Mistholt is a city of mystery, but to its people, the mist is as much a part of their home as the sea itself.
