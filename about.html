About Halidom

The World’s Origin:

This website has been created for the soul purpose of documenting something that originally began as a Dungeons & Dragons campaign. Over time, the stories, characters, locations, and events that shaped the campaign have become the foundation for an ever-expanding fantasy world. As the campaign ended I wished to continue the setting and turn it into a whole world which I could use as a baseline for my other magicians and stories going forward. This website serves as a central hub for collecting, sharing, and expanding upon the lore of that world. Currently this website stores limited information as most of the world is ever changing, it’s initial job will be to store and show information as I have it.

The Creator:

I am Robert Risch a 26 year old DVC, DFD student at UWM. I have a bachelors in automation and have worked in the field for 6 years now. I came back to school wanting to expand my skill set and hopefully move off of the arduous shop floor and into a more conformable role in the future. I am a huge gamer, reader and digital artist in my down time and if not obvious Dungeons and Dragons is a big hobby of mine. While i love making stories through my friends and the game, I want to make the foundation to be able to make larger stories.
