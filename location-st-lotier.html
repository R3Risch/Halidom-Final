St. Lotier — The City of the Eternal Flame

The Sacred City of Sacrifice and Salvation

Built upon the ashes of a devastating tragedy, St. Lotier is a revered religious city named in honor of Saint Lotier, a selfless cleric who sacrificed his life to save countless civilians during a catastrophic fire that once consumed the city. According to legend, Lotier remained within the burning streets long after others had fled, guiding the lost to safety until the flames finally claimed him. In the centuries since, the city has become a sanctuary for pilgrims, healers, and devoted followers of the gods, with grand cathedrals, sacred monuments, and countless shrines commemorating his sacrifice. Though its streets are now peaceful and its architecture magnificent, the memory of the Great Fire remains deeply woven into the city's identity, serving as an eternal reminder that true faith is measured not by devotion alone, but by what one is willing to sacrifice for others.
