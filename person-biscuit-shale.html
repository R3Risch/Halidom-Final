Biscuit Shale

Gender: Male

Race: Human

Age: 37

Profession: Competitive Sprinter

"They told me I'd never run again. Funny thing about lightning, though. It doesn't need legs to move."

Biscuit Shale was born with two things: an unfortunate name and a pair of legs that never seemed to tire. Growing up in a modest farming village, Biscuit quickly discovered that he was faster than just about anyone around him. What began as friendly races through the fields eventually became a promising career as a competitive runner. By his early twenties, Biscuit had earned a reputation as one of the fastest runners in the region, competing in prestigious races and earning the nickname "The Stray Bolt." For Biscuit, running wasn't just a sport; it was the one thing that made him feel truly free.

That all changed during the final stretch of the most important race of his career. With the finish line in sight, a violent thunderstorm rolled overhead. As Biscuit pushed himself toward victory, a bolt of lightning struck the track directly in front of him. The resulting blast threw him several feet, leaving his right leg permanently damaged and ending his career overnight. Although he survived, Biscuit struggled to accept that the one thing he had dedicated his life to had been taken from him in an instant.

During his recovery, Biscuit became obsessed with understanding the lightning that had ruined his life. What started as resentment slowly turned into fascination. He began studying books on storms, magical currents, and the elemental forces of nature. Eventually, he sought out a reclusive wizard who specialized in manipulating electricity. Despite having no formal magical education, Biscuit proved to be a surprisingly dedicated student. He approached spellcasting the same way he had approached running: through relentless practice, repetition, and an almost unhealthy desire to improve.

Years later, Biscuit has traded his racing shoes for a spellbook and his place on the track for a life of adventuring. Although his injured leg prevents him from running like he once did, his magic allows him to experience that familiar rush of speed once again. He dreams of someday developing a spell that will let him move faster than he ever could before his injury. Until then, Biscuit travels the world seeking forgotten magical knowledge, testing the limits of lightning magic, and occasionally challenging strangers to footraces he knows he probably shouldn't.
