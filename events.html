World Event Intro

25 years ago a new god was born by mortal hands, in response the countless gods held trial. Welcome the new god as one our us or banish the once mortal to the outer plains and prevent other mortals from following in their footsteps. As the gods quickly picked sides the trial quickly forgot about the fledgling deity and began opening old wounds. Holy war broke out once again as Altea, home to Xirvia and ground zero to his ascension became a battlefield. 3 years passed as soil crumbled and the skies scorched. It wasn't until the fall of the capital city of Solstice that the Ordinal Deities finally stepped in, stopping the war.

A second divine trial was summoned, but not by the Ordinal Deities' call, a single small-time god sat at the head of the meeting. Levi the god of games, play, trickery and luck, the only god that stayed neutral in the face of war struck a deal with the Ordinal gods and forced a ceasefire. The only true quote gods shared with mortals was this.

“You creatures who have fought not 1 but now 2 wars with strength and death, who built a tower of corpses that rise above the very skies and call yourselves wise, tell me this: What is the difference between you and the so-called dumb mortals you rule. Immortality? No Intelligence? No, the only difference between you and mortals is your inability to change. Your war games tire, strategies dulled, no progress has been made, only death and destruction. And so I propose a new game.

Thus divinity changed, The 3 ordinal gods ripped themselves apart and created 3 divine walls, and new sanctions. 9 chosen Inheritor gods remain, as divinity is filtered and separated from Halidom itself. A new pantheon made up of 4 gods from each side of the last conflict, the Astral Court and the Ahsen Meridian, and a single minor god left as a neutral party, Levi the god of play. Divinity above an unspecified amount has been completely cut off from the world, the remaining gods can only interact by permanently spending their own causality.

The old seals are gone, divine secrets revealed, forbidden lands opened, old world treasures now unlocked as the halidom is now unfiltered by divinity. While altea is recovering the rest of the world is now open to a new golden age of discovery. Adventures are now flourishing as new beasts roam free of old shackles and ancient dungeons are now flourishing with untouched loot. Mortals are no longer having their hands held by divine law. Only a few gods remain to observe and guide when truly needed, so what will you do?
