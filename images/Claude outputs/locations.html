Altea — A Land of Magic and Forgotten Wonders

Steeped in magic and teeming with life, Altea is a continent of untamed wilderness, ancient mysteries, and long-forgotten wonders. From sprawling forests and towering mountain ranges to strange, magically altered landscapes, the continent boasts an extraordinary diversity of life, where the natural and supernatural have become deeply intertwined.

With the recent redistribution of the Inheritor Gods, long-sealed ruins and technological marvels of civilizations past have begun to emerge once more. Ancient machinery stirs beneath the earth, forgotten cities open their gates, and secrets once lost to time now await discovery. As scholars, adventurers, and ambitious kingdoms rush to uncover these wonders, Altea finds itself on the brink of a new age of exploration.

Yet, beneath its breathtaking beauty and endless opportunities lies a continent whose greatest mysteries have only just begun to awaken.
