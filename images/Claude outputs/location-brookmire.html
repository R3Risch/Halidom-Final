Brookmire — The City of Education

Situated along the shores of a large island just off the coast of Altea, Brookmire is a peaceful coastal city renowned as one of the continent's greatest centers of knowledge and education. With its salt-worn stone buildings, bustling harbors, and seaside academies, the city welcomes scholars, inventors, philosophers, and aspiring mages from across the world. Its winding streets are lined with bookstores, research institutions, and quiet gardens, while open-air lectures and public debates overlook the crashing waves. Each year, Brookmire hosts the prestigious Conference of Minds, drawing brilliant thinkers from distant lands to exchange ideas, unveil discoveries, and challenge established beliefs.
