Claude Wallace

Gender: Male

Race: Palid Elf

Age: 383

Profession: Cleric of Celeste

"May your final breath be gentle, and may the Boreal Mother welcome you among her stars."

Claude Wallace has spent his whole life in the northernmost limits of Altea. He lived in a small community of pallid elfs just like himself and was raised as a follower of Celeste. As he grew older he found that he had an especially strong connection to his god and was soon ordained as a cleric. His parents encouraged him to take a pilgrimage as a cleric to help and heal wherever he could, but also help those who have passed on and to help their families.

On this pilgrimage is where he met who would eventually become his wife, Quilen Sontovis. Quilen was high elf royalty, and Cluade was sanctioned by the family to help their ailing house mother. Over the course of the house mothers rehabilitation Quilen told her father he wanted to marry Claude, but her father refused to let it happen. Eventually after the house mother was well again Claude left for home once again.

Shortly after making it home to the north, Claude found that Quilen had followed him and she told her of her plans to marry him. They were soon married and eventually had a daughter Joyra. They lived happy lives together with the community around them growing smaller, and Cluade leaving every so often to perform works in Celeste's name.

On one such trip he returned home to find his small village abandoned and his family gone. With no sign of struggle and no leads on where his family is, Claude is traveling the world to try and find his family again, although she suspects his in-laws.
