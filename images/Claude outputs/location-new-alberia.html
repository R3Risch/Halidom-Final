New Alberia — The Gateway to the North

Nestled between two towering mountain peaks along the northern reaches of Altea, New Alberia is a resilient mountain city known as the Gateway to the North. Serving as the primary passage between the continent's temperate heartlands and the frozen wilderness beyond, the city is a vital crossroads for merchants, explorers, and travelers brave enough to venture northward. At its center lies the remarkable Everstill Lake, a vast body of crystal-clear water that refuses to freeze, even during the harshest winters. Fed by mysterious currents beneath the mountains, the lake has sustained generations of settlers and inspired countless legends about its supernatural origins. Surrounded by snow-covered peaks, towering pines, and fortified stone walls, New Alberia is a place where northern traditions meet southern influences, and where the warmth of its people stands in stark contrast to the unforgiving wilderness beyond.
