Cordelia — The Iron Bastion

Carved into the towering cliffs of a volcanic mountain range, Cordelia is a massive fortified city renowned for its unmatched steel production, legendary forges, and imposing military architecture. Built across layers of black volcanic stone, the city is a sprawling network of fortresses, industrial districts, and underground passageways, illuminated by the orange glow of countless furnaces. Rivers of molten rock flow through carefully engineered channels, powering the foundries that produce some of the finest weapons, armor, and machinery in all of Altea. Generations of blacksmiths, engineers, and soldiers have shaped Cordelia into an industrial powerhouse, where craftsmanship is considered as honorable as combat. Though the city is often blanketed in smoke and ash, its people take immense pride in their resilience, believing that just as steel is strengthened by fire, so too are those who call Cordelia home.
