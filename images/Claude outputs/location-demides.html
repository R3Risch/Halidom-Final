Demides — The City of Lanterns

The Northernmost City of Altea

Standing at the edge of the known world, Demides is the northernmost city of Altea, a remote settlement trapped beneath an eternal winter and an endless night. Known as the City of Lanterns, Demides is famous for the thousands of lights that illuminate its snow-covered streets, towering buildings, and frozen canals. With the sun having long since vanished beyond the horizon, its people have gone to extraordinary lengths to keep their home illuminated, developing elaborate networks of enchanted lanterns, enormous braziers, and magical beacons that turn the city into a sea of warm light against the surrounding darkness. Despite the bitter cold and isolation, Demides remains a surprisingly lively community, where hospitality, cooperation, and resilience are essential to survival. Above the city, brilliant auroras dance across the star-filled sky, offering a rare glimpse of natural beauty in a land where dawn has become little more than a distant memory.
