Halidom

Pocket Palewood

Gender: Male

Race: Changling

Age: 23

Profession: Doppelganger for Hire

“Sorry, I refuse to engage in a battle of wits with an unarmed opponent.”

Born Malis Valmosa, Pocket Palewood was the firstborn son of Alfred and Celia Valmosa, members of a minor noble house in Akaastra. Despite both his parents being human, Malis was born a changeling, something his family considered a disgrace to their reputation. Throughout his childhood, he was forced to maintain the appearance of a perfect human child, facing punishment whenever his true nature was revealed. When Malis was twelve, his younger brother Carlan was born human and immediately named heir to the family, leaving Malis stripped of his noble privileges and largely forgotten. With his parents' attention focused on Carlan, Malis found freedom wandering the streets of Akaastra, observing its people and studying their appearances and mannerisms. By sixteen, he began using his changeling abilities professionally under the alias "Pocket," offering his services as a body double for anyone willing to pay. What began as small jobs avoiding awkward dates and tedious obligations eventually grew into a successful business, with merchants and lesser nobles hiring him to attend social gatherings, meetings, and even potentially dangerous situations in their place.

By the age of twenty-three, Pocket had earned a considerable reputation as a professional doppelganger, aided by the mysterious development of magical abilities beyond those of an ordinary changeling. Among these powers was the ability to summon Herschel, a Sphinx of Wonder who became his familiar and companion. Despite his success, his relationship with his family had only worsened, with even his younger brother adopting their parents' contempt toward him. One day, while cleaning his father's study, Malis discovered an old contract signed by Alfred and Celia Valmosa. The document promised to alter their fates in exchange for greater power and social standing, though the cost was left deliberately vague, described only as something that would become obvious. At the bottom was the signature of a mysterious individual named Nilokaras. Suspecting that the agreement might explain his unusual birth and emerging magical abilities, Malis finally found a reason to abandon the family that had rejected him. Taking the contract and his self-made adventuring equipment, he left Akaastra behind, abandoning the Valmosa name and fully embracing his identity as Pocket Palewood. Accompanied by Herschel, he now travels the world searching for Nilokaras and the truth behind his existence, determined to discover whether his life was simply the price of his parents' ambitions or something far greater.
