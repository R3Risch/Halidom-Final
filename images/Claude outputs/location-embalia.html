Embalia — The City of Traditions

Rising from the golden sands of Altea's vast deserts, Embalia is an ancient city built around a colossal inverted spire that descends deep into the earth. Known as the City of Traditions, Embalia is a place where centuries-old customs, sacred rituals, and ancestral teachings remain deeply woven into everyday life. Its sandstone buildings, shaded bazaars, and winding streets surround the enormous spire, whose mysterious origins predate even the oldest surviving records. While the world beyond its walls continues to change, Embalia remains steadfast in preserving its heritage, believing that the wisdom of the past is essential to navigating the future. Yet, with the recent redistribution of the Inheritor Gods, strange activity has begun stirring within the spire's forgotten depths, threatening to unravel traditions that have endured for generations.
