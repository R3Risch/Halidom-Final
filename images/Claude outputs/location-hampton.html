Hampton — The City of Festivals

The Woodland Heart of Celebration

Nestled along the winding banks of a gentle creek deep within Altea's lush forests, Hampton is a charming woodland city renowned for its lively festivals, colorful traditions, and welcoming atmosphere. Surrounded by towering trees, wildflower meadows, and crystal-clear streams, the city is a patchwork of timber-framed homes, moss-covered stone bridges, and bustling market squares decorated with ribbons and lanterns. Known throughout Altea as the City of Festivals, Hampton hosts celebrations throughout the year, honoring everything from seasonal changes and bountiful harvests to music, art, and local folklore. Its people believe that life is best lived in good company, and visitors often find themselves swept into festivities regardless of the occasion. Beneath its cheerful exterior, Hampton is a city deeply connected to the surrounding forest, where ancient customs and a touch of woodland magic ensure that there is always something worth celebrating.
